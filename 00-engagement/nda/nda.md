# 1. Purpose

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("NDA") establishes the confidentiality obligations applicable to information exchanged, accessed, created or discovered during the **crAPI Application Security Assessment**.

The purpose of this agreement is to ensure that sensitive technical, security, operational and business information obtained during the engagement is protected against unauthorized use or disclosure.

This NDA is intended to operate as a **mutual confidentiality agreement**, meaning that confidentiality obligations apply to information disclosed by either party during the engagement.

# 2. Parties and Engagement

For the purposes of this agreement:

**Client Representative:** Mr. Mario  
**Application Security Expert:** Mr. Wario

Mr. Wario has been authorized to conduct the crAPI Application Security Assessment against the approved UAT environment in accordance with the separate:

- Scope of Security Assessment;
- Rules of Engagement;
- Authorization to Test;
- Evidence Handling Plan.

The parties acknowledge that the assessment may require access to sensitive technical and security information.

# 3. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" includes any non-public information disclosed, accessed, observed, generated or obtained in connection with the assessment.

This may include, but is not limited to:

- system architecture;
- network information;
- IP addresses and service information;
- application architecture;
- API endpoints;
- undocumented or internal APIs;
- source-code excerpts;
- configuration files;
- environment variables;
- credentials;
- passwords;
- API keys;
- authentication tokens;
- session cookies;
- private keys;
- database information;
- personally identifiable information;
- application logs;
- security controls;
- security weaknesses;
- vulnerability findings;
- proof-of-concept material;
- screenshots;
- packet captures;
- HTTP requests and responses;
- penetration-testing results;
- internal business information;
- remediation plans;
- assessment reports.

Information does not need to be specifically marked "Confidential" in order to be protected if its nature or context reasonably indicates that it should be treated as confidential.

# 4. Confidentiality Obligations

Each party agrees to:

- protect Confidential Information using reasonable security measures;
- use Confidential Information only for purposes related to the authorized engagement;
- restrict access to persons who reasonably require the information;
- prevent unauthorized disclosure, copying or distribution;
- avoid placing sensitive information on unapproved systems or services;
- promptly notify the other party if unauthorized disclosure or loss of Confidential Information is identified.

Mr. Wario must not use client information for any purpose unrelated to the assessment without explicit approval.

Mr. Mario must similarly protect any confidential assessor information, tools, methodology or proprietary material provided during the engagement.

# 5. Permitted Use and Disclosure

Confidential Information may be used only where necessary to:

- conduct the authorized security assessment;
- validate security findings;
- perform remediation;
- prepare assessment reports;
- discuss High or Critical findings;
- perform agreed retesting;
- respond to security incidents associated with the engagement;
- satisfy applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

Confidential Information must not be disclosed to unrelated third parties without prior authorization, except where disclosure is required by applicable law.

Where third-party consultants, legal advisers, auditors or other authorized personnel require access, they should be subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.

# 6. Information Not Considered Confidential

Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that can be demonstrated to:

- have been lawfully known before disclosure;
- already be publicly available through lawful means;
- become publicly available without breach of this agreement;
- be lawfully received from an authorized third party without confidentiality restrictions;
- have been independently developed without use of Confidential Information.

A vulnerability becoming publicly known does not automatically permit disclosure of client-specific evidence, architecture, affected systems or remediation information.

# 7. Security Assessment Evidence and Data Handling

All evidence collected during the engagement will be handled in accordance with the approved **Evidence Handling Plan**.

This includes requirements relating to:

- approved testing workstations;
- evidence storage;
- access control;
- sensitive information;
- evidence transfer;
- customer data;
- retention;
- secure destruction.

Assessment evidence should be stored only on systems approved for the engagement.

Personal cloud services, personal email accounts, public repositories, public file-sharing services and other unapproved storage locations must not be used for confidential engagement data.

Where the client requires use of a client-managed workstation, evidence must remain within the client-controlled environment unless explicit approval is provided for transfer.

# 8. Security Findings and Vulnerability Information

Security findings identified during the assessment are considered Confidential Information.

This includes:

- vulnerability descriptions;
- affected endpoints;
- proof-of-concept requests;
- exploitation details;
- screenshots;
- attack paths;
- credentials or tokens involved;
- remediation information.

High and Critical findings must be communicated to Mr. Mario as soon as reasonably possible following validation, in accordance with the Rules of Engagement.

Security findings must not be publicly disclosed or shared with unrelated parties without client authorization.

Any responsible-disclosure or external notification activity requires separate agreement between the parties.

# 9. Retention, Return and Destruction

Assessment information and evidence will be retained only for the period required to complete:

- testing;
- reporting;
- remediation support;
- agreed retesting;
- engagement closure.

The agreed raw evidence destruction date is:

**31 October 2026**

Evidence destruction will be performed in accordance with the Evidence Handling Plan.

The following may be retained as formal engagement records where required:

- finalized Scope;
- finalized Rules of Engagement;
- Authorization to Test;
- this NDA;
- Evidence Handling Plan;
- final assessment report;
- final retest report;
- agreed sanitized engagement records.

If either party requests earlier return or destruction of Confidential Information, the request should be honored where reasonably possible and where no legal or contractual retention requirement prevents it.

# 10. Legal and Regulatory Requirements

Both parties agree that information collected or handled during the engagement will be processed in accordance with applicable laws and regulations of the **United Arab Emirates**.

Where applicable, this includes obligations relating to:

- confidentiality;
- privacy;
- personal-data protection;
- unauthorized access;
- cybercrime;
- electronic information;
- contractual obligations.

If either party becomes legally required to disclose Confidential Information, that party should, where legally permitted, notify the other party before disclosure and limit disclosure to the minimum legally required information.

Nothing in this agreement authorizes activity prohibited by applicable UAE law.

# 11. Term and Survival

This NDA becomes effective upon approval by both parties.

The confidentiality obligations remain in force throughout the engagement and continue after the technical assessment has concluded.

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, confidentiality obligations relating to ordinary Confidential Information will remain in effect for **three years following engagement closure**.

Obligations concerning the following continue for as long as the information remains sensitive or legally protected:

- passwords;
- private keys;
- credentials;
- personal data;
- customer data;
- trade secrets;
- other information protected by applicable law.

# 12. Liability and No Transfer of Rights

Disclosure of Confidential Information under this agreement does not transfer ownership, intellectual-property rights or any other rights to the receiving party.

Information remains the property of the party entitled to it.

Nothing in this NDA:

- grants ownership of client systems or data to Mr. Wario;
- grants the client ownership of Mr. Wario's pre-existing tools, techniques or intellectual property;
- authorizes testing beyond the approved Scope and Authorization to Test.

Each party remains responsible for protecting Confidential Information while it is under that party's control.

# 13. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This agreement will be governed by and interpreted in accordance with the applicable laws of the **United Arab Emirates**.

The parties should first attempt to resolve any disagreement relating to this NDA through good-faith discussion.

Where a dispute cannot be resolved between the parties, it will be referred to the competent courts or authorities having jurisdiction within the United Arab Emirates, unless another dispute-resolution mechanism is separately agreed in writing.

# 14. Relationship to Other Engagement Documents

This NDA governs confidentiality and protection of information.

It does not replace the technical or operational engagement documents.

The following documents should be read together:

| Document | Primary Purpose |
|---|---|
| Scope of Security Assessment | Defines what may be tested |
| Rules of Engagement | Defines how testing may be performed |
| Authorization to Test | Provides formal permission to perform testing |
| Evidence Handling Plan | Defines how evidence is stored, handled and destroyed |
| Non-Disclosure Agreement | Defines confidentiality obligations |

If an ambiguity exists between documents, the parties should clarify the matter before performing the affected activity.

Nothing within this NDA expands the authorized technical scope.
